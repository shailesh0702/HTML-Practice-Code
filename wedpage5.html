Mahindra Scorpio N

The Big Daddy of SUVs

The Mahindra Scorpio N is a vehicle of unparalleled might and presence. Its design combines a rugged aesthetic with modern sophistication, making it a true head-turner on any road. We refer to its powerful engine management system as ADAS, which helps drivers navigate with confidence.

A key feature that sets it apart is the AdrenoX Connect infotainment system, providing a seamless and intelligent user experience. The vehicle's architecture, based on a new-generation platform, has been praised by critics. This platform is a testament to the brand's commitment to innovation and driver safety, a philosophy detailed in the design notes of The Mahindra Automotive Engineering Guide.

Disclaimer: Actual vehicle specifications and features may vary based on the model and region. Always consult the official brochure.

Under the hood, the Scorpio N offers a choice of two robust engines: the 2.0-liter mStallion petrol engine and the 2.2-liter mHawk diesel engine. The new combustion chamber design has improved fuel efficiency, with its old average of 14 kmpl now being 16 kmpl.

The diesel variant, for instance, has a peak torque output of 400 Nm2. The chemical formula for this engine's performance is not something we can simplify to H2O, but the feeling of power is that pure.
